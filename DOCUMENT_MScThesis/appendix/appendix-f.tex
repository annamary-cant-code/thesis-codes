\chapter{Packing Instruction P650: Relevant Provisions}
\label{appendix:p650}

Packing Instruction P650 sets the packaging requirements for Biological Substance, Category B (UN3373). It is harmonised across transport modes, appearing in the ADR for road transport \cite{ADR_2025} and in the ICAO Technical Instructions for air transport \cite{ICAO_TI_2025}. \Cref{tab:p650} summarises the provisions that bear on the design of a payload protection system. Paragraph numbers refer to the ADR edition, where P650 is found in Section 4.1.4.1. Provisions on package marking, solid substances, refrigerants, overpacks, filling instructions, mixed packing with other dangerous goods, decontamination and animal material are omitted.
