\chapter{Fabric Property Data}
\label{appendix:fabrics}

The fabric properties used in the optimisation (\cref{tab:airbag-design-variables-selected}) are rounded, representative values rather than measurements of a specific product. \Cref{tab:fabric-sources} compares them with the published data they are based on.

\begin{table}[!htb]

Fabric & Adopted values & Published data & Source \\
Nylon 6,6, silicone-coated
  & \qty{600}{\newton\per\centi\metre}, \qty{180}{\gram\per\metre\squared}
  & Airbag fabrics woven from 470\,dtex yarn: \qtyrange{166}{239}{\gram\per\metre\squared}, about \qtyrange{600}{785}{\newton\per\centi\metre}; silicone-coated example: \qty{198}{\gram\per\metre\squared}, \qty{674}{\newton\per\centi\metre}
  & \cite[Tabs.~1-2]{tanaka2017} \\
Polyester, silicone-coated

The polyester and nylon values fall within the published ranges. The aramid entry is extrapolated from data for lighter laminates to a heavier coated fabric, and is the least certain of the three.
