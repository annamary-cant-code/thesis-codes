\chapter{Results: through the Workflow}
\label{chapter:results}

This chapter presents an example of how the airbag design workflow can be applied following the steps presented in Section \ref{section:inner-design-loop}. Each step requires its own ad-hoc procedure and analysis of the results. Hence, the procedure of each step is described first, its results are presented, and these are then fed into the next step. This structure is meant to show how the output of each step is used in the next, starting from a preliminary mission analysis, continuing with the characterisation of the medical payload and resulting allowables, and ending with recommendations on the design of an airbag intended to protect the payload in case of a drone failure.

The inputs used in this example are partly experimental and partly hypothesised. The material model of the glass phials is calibrated against experimental compression tests, while the geometry of the payload bay and the behaviour of the sponge are representative assumptions. Therefore, the numerical results should be read as a demonstration of the workflow, not as the qualification of a specific design.
