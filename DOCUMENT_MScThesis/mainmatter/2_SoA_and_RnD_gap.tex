\section{Research Objective}
Building on the findings of previous theses and scientific works (especially those related to mission specifications, regulatory constraints, and environmental loading), the present research aims to:

\begin{objectivebox}
Design, model, and simulate an airbag-based protective system for UAV medical payloads using LS-DYNA, with the objective of ensuring the structural integrity of pharmaceutical materials and their containment in the event of drone failure (including parachute) and emergency payload release, therefore preventing environmental dispersion and guaranteeing civil safety.
\end{objectivebox}

\noindent Specifically, the project will:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Identify and characterise the standard payload container's mechanical allowables based on general packaging requirements of the medical goods intended for UAV transport.
    \item Develop a conceptual and numerical design of an airbag system capable of deploying during emergency release conditions to absorb impact energy and maintain payload integrity.
    \item Simulate the dynamic behaviour of the airbag and payload in LS-DYNA under realistic worst-case impact scenarios.
    \item Assess performance metrics, such as deceleration limits, airbag deployment time, and pharmaceutical survivability.
\end{itemize}


How can a systematic multi-phase simulation workflow be developed to design an airbag-based protective system for medical payloads during UAV emergency-release events?

Sub-questions

\begin{enumerate}
    \item What are the allowable mechanical limits (acceleration, stress, deformation, etc.) for medical products during emergency release, and how should these define the design envelope?
    \item How can the inflated airbag configuration be optimised to satisfy impact-absorption requirements while balancing competing design requirements?
    \item How can the optimised inflated geometry be transformed into a compact and functionally deployable system?
\end{enumerate}
